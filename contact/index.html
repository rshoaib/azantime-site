Questions, corrections and feedback

Azan Time is made by one person, Rizwan. Every message is read by me, and I usually reply within two or three days.

Email

segmentbi@gmail.com

What to include

  • A wrong prayer time: the city, the date, the prayer, and the timetable you compare against (for example your mosque's).
  • An app problem: your phone model and Android version, and what you expected to happen.
  • A privacy request: what you would like to know or have removed. The app keeps your data on your phone, so there is usually nothing on a server to delete; I will confirm either way.
  • A new city: the city name and country.

Prefer the app store? You can also leave a review on Google Play; I reply there too.
