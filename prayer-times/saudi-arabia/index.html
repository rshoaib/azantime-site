About Prayer Times in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia uses the Umm al-Qura calculation method, developed by Umm al-Qura University in Makkah. Fajr is set at 18.5° below the horizon, and Isha is calculated as 90 minutes after Maghrib (extended to 120 minutes during Ramadan). This method is the official standard for Masjid al-Haram and Masjid an-Nabawi.

Asr in Saudi Arabia follows the Shafi'i / Maliki / Hanbali calculation (shadow = object length + midday shadow), which is standard across most of the Arab world and earlier than the Hanafi Asr used in South Asia — in Riyadh by about 81 minutes on 15 June and 44 minutes on 15 December.

For pilgrims performing Umrah or Hajj, the Azan Time app shows live prayer countdowns in Makkah and Madinah, plus an offline Qibla compass pointing directly to the Kaaba.

How the cities in Saudi Arabia compare

Earliest and latest Fajr and Maghrib across the year (standard time) and the great-circle qibla bearing, calculated for each city’s coordinates with the method shown on its page. On 15 June a fast from Fajr to Maghrib lasts 15 h 11 min in Riyadh but 14 h 54 min in Makkah — a difference of 17 minutes inside one country. Dhuhr on 15 March is at 12:02 in Riyadh and 12:31 in Madinah: the same prayer, 29 minutes apart on the clock, because of where each city sits in its time zone.

Questions about prayer times in Saudi Arabia

When is the longest fast in Saudi Arabia?

Around the June solstice. In Makkah, a fast from Fajr to Maghrib on 15 June lasts 14 h 54 min; on 15 December it lasts 12 h 12 min. Ramadan moves about 11 days earlier each year, so the fast length you meet depends on the year.

Which way is the Qibla from Saudi Arabia?

Between 176.2° and 243.8° from true north, depending on the city (see the table). These are true-north bearings; a phone compass shows magnetic north unless it corrects for declination.

Which calculation method is used for Saudi Arabia?

Umm al-Qura University, Makkah, with the standard Asr time. Each city page states how many minutes apart the standard and Hanafi Asr times are, and names its method.
