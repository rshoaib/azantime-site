About Prayer Times in Morocco

Morocco predominantly follows the Maliki madhhab and uses the Ministry of Habous and Islamic Affairs calculation — Fajr at 18° and Isha at 17° below the horizon. Asr follows the Shafi'i / Maliki standard (shadow = object length + midday shadow). Times are also available via the Muslim World League method which produces nearly identical results.

Morocco observes permanent UTC+1 (GMT+1 year-round), having dropped seasonal DST in 2018. During Ramadan, however, the country temporarily reverts to GMT for the holy month only. Azan Time handles this edge case automatically.

The Qibla from Morocco points roughly east — about 94° from Casablanca and 91° from Marrakech. Install Azan Time for live adhan, offline Qibla and a Hijri calendar — entièrement gratuit.

How the cities in Morocco compare

Earliest and latest Fajr and Maghrib across the year (standard time) and the great-circle qibla bearing, calculated for each city’s coordinates with the method shown on its page. On 15 June a fast from Fajr to Maghrib lasts 16 h 06 min in Casablanca but 15 h 51 min in Marrakech — a difference of 15 minutes inside one country. Dhuhr on 15 March is at 13:39 in Casablanca and 13:41 in Marrakech: the same prayer, 2 minutes apart on the clock, because of where each city sits in its time zone.

Questions about prayer times in Morocco

When is the longest fast in Morocco?

Around the June solstice. In Casablanca, a fast from Fajr to Maghrib on 15 June lasts 16 h 06 min; on 15 December it lasts 11 h 25 min. Ramadan moves about 11 days earlier each year, so the fast length you meet depends on the year.

Which way is the Qibla from Morocco?

Between 91.4° and 93.7° from true north, depending on the city (see the table). These are true-north bearings; a phone compass shows magnetic north unless it corrects for declination.

Which calculation method is used for Morocco?

Ministry of Habous and Islamic Affairs, with the standard Asr time. Each city page states how many minutes apart the standard and Hanafi Asr times are, and names its method.
